aka ./survive --list — the survival guide series. One front door, four flavors of panic. Pick yours.

what this is, and where to start

A welcome package for new joiners and a mid-panic reference for everyone else. Each module is one self-contained page covering a survival skill of the trade — your machine, your git history, your pipeline. New here? Read them in this order:

01 setup→ 02 git→ 03 pipelines→ 04 spark (soon)

Each one stands alone — but they link forward: the setup guide's inner loop becomes the pipeline guide's outer loop, and the pipeline guide's testing chapter is what the Spark guide will assume you know.
